Checking prices all day is a job. Let it be the app's job.

If you hold shares in several companies, each one has its own buying price, quantity and the price at which you would like to sell. Keeping all of that in your head, or in a spreadsheet you have to refresh, means targets get missed.

StockSentry keeps every holding in one place. You enter what you own and the range you are hoping for. It does the arithmetic, watches the price, and raises a flag when your range is reached. What you do next is entirely your call.

What you can use it for

See how every holding is doing

Invested amount, current value, profit or loss and percentage return, calculated for each stock and for the whole portfolio.

Set a range, not just a number

Choose a lowest and highest target price for each stock.

Get told when it matters

A dashboard alert appears when a price enters your range, with an optional email.

Know when a price is old

If a fresh price cannot be fetched, the last one is kept and clearly marked as stale.

Keep your data to yourself

Holdings are stored in a database file on your own computer. There is no broker login and no way to place an order from the app.

How it works

  1. Add a holding

    Enter the company, symbol, exchange, quantity and the price you paid.

  2. Pick your target range

    Tell StockSentry the lowest and highest price you would be happy to see.

  3. Let it watch

    Prices are checked at regular intervals while the app is running.

  4. Decide for yourself

    When the range is reached you get an alert. Any buying or selling is done by you, elsewhere.
